In this work, we investigated how the brain processes temporal information of visual events and integrates it with spatial information. By developing novel neuronal response models that combine these two sensory dimensions, we showed that duration tuning changes along the visual hierarchy, as does its link with spatial tuning. In early visual areas, duration tuning is monotonic and localised in populations that code for stimulus position. In downstream visual regions, duration tuning becomes Gaussian and interacts with spatial tuning by eliciting common neural responses and following shared topographic principles.
