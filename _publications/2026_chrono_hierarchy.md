In this work, by combining a high-density brain parcellation with a mechanistic model of temporal coding, we characterise how duration tuning changes along the cortical hierarchy and whether it relates to perception. We show three stages of temporal processing that recruit different cortical areas and entail different tuning properties. Among these stages, the one occurring in frontal regions is tightly linked to perceived, rather than physical, durations.
